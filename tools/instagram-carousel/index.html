Instagram carousel white border tool

Put portrait, landscape and square photos on one white canvas with an even border, so Instagram doesn't crop your carousel and mixed photos sit in the same frame like gallery prints.

Free, no sign-up. Everything runs in your browser and your images never leave your device. This page uses Rybbit for cookieless pageview analytics only (privacy policy).

How to add white borders to an Instagram carousel

  1. Drop your photos onto the page, or pick them from your device. Drag the slides to change their order.
  2. Leave the aspect ratio on Auto, or force 4:5, 1:1, 1.91:1 or 3:4.
  3. Move the padding slider until the border looks right. Click any slide to swipe through a preview.
  4. Choose 1080 as the export width for files sized exactly for Instagram, then download the ZIP.
  5. In Instagram, select the slides in their numbered order. They already share one shape, so nothing gets cropped.

How Auto picks the canvas shape

Instagram makes every slide in a carousel the same shape and crops anything that doesn't fit. Auto looks at every photo in the set and picks the ratio within Instagram's 1.91:1 to 4:5 range that leaves the least empty space overall. Three tall photos and one wide one end up on a 4:5 canvas, with white above and below the wide photo. A set of mostly wide photos gets a landscape canvas instead.

The canvas is the smallest one that holds every photo at its native resolution, so no photo is scaled up. Photos taller than 4:5 or wider than 1.91:1 get extra white on the sides or top and bottom, so they still fit without being cropped.

Questions

Why not just use Instagram's Mixed option?

Instagram's Mixed option keeps each photo's shape, but it fills the empty space with a background that follows the app's light or dark theme, and you can't control its color or size. This tool bakes an even white border into every image, so the carousel looks the same for every viewer.

How do I post portrait and landscape photos in one carousel without cropping?

Add all the photos here, keep the aspect ratio on Auto, and download the ZIP. Every slide comes out at the same canvas size with the whole photo inside it, so Instagram has nothing to crop. Upload the slides in the numbered order.

What size should Instagram carousel images be?

Instagram stores posts at 1080 pixels wide, with an aspect ratio between 1.91:1 (1080 × 566) and 4:5 (1080 × 1350). The app also accepts 3:4 (1080 × 1440). Every slide in a carousel should share one ratio. Choose the 1080 export width here for files sized exactly for Instagram.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The images are decoded, padded and encoded by your browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.

How many photos can I add?

Up to 20, which is the most Instagram allows in one carousel. The tool also warns you when a slide is larger than 8 MB.

How wide should the white border be?

Padding is a percentage of the shared photo area, from 0 to 20%. Around 5% reads as a thin frame and around 10% looks like a matted gallery print. At 0% the photos still share one canvas, with white only where their shapes differ.

Should I export JPEG or PNG?

JPEG at 95% quality suits photos and keeps files small. PNG is lossless and better for screenshots, text or flat graphics, but the files are much larger.

Can I use a border color other than white?

Not yet, the padding is always white. If you want another color, open an issue on GitHub and say what you would use it for.
